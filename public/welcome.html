How it works

Set it up once. Takes about five minutes.

1

Add RotBlock to your home screen

Open rotblock.vercel.app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

2

Turn on the blocker

Install the free Userscripts app and add RotBlock's blocker. The app walks you through it.

3

Open your apps from RotBlock

Pick what disappears with a switch. Delete the original apps so nothing slips through.

Scan to get it on your phone.

RotBlock lives on your iPhone. Point your camera at the code and it opens in Safari, ready to add to your home screen.
